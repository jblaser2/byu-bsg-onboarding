!!! info "Once you're officially in the group"
    The primary way the group communicates is **Zulip** — similar to Slack, with channels, topics
    within each channel, and direct messages.

    1. [Create a Zulip account](https://zulip.com/).

       [Who's Who](contacts.md)) to add you to the group's Zulip organization.
